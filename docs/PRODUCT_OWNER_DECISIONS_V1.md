**D-11 decision record (2026-11-21):** The product owner approved Option A: every balance-affecting financial mutation must execute through PostgreSQL RPC. Browser/client code must not directly insert, update or delete ledger/financial facts. RPCs must validate `auth.uid()`, active workspace membership, operation authorization, same-workspace references, input constraints, idempotency and concurrency as applicable; related transaction and journal writes must commit atomically. **D-12 decision record (2026-11-21):** The product owner approved **Option A**. Every monetary value in JSON request/response payloads is represented as a base-10 decimal integer string in currency minor units, regardless of magnitude. TypeScript validates the string and converts it to `bigint` before monetary arithmetic. JSON monetary values must never be encoded as JSON numbers. Currency exponent is read from currency metadata and must not be assumed to be two. FX rates and ratios use decimal-safe representations and arithmetic; formatted currency strings are presentation-only. Examples: IDR 125,000 → `"125000"`; USD 100.00 (exponent 2) → `"10000"`; JPY 500 (exponent 0) → `"500"`. This approves the wire representation, not every RPC payload schema or the remaining G2 data dictionary and implementation details.

**D-13 decision record (2026-11-21):** The product owner approved **Option A — SQL Views** as POCKA V1's initial balance/read model. Account balances and reporting read models are derived from the authoritative ledger through SQL views; no separately stored balance/cache table is introduced by default. Materialized views or caches may be considered only when measured performance evidence demonstrates a need and reconciliation tests prove they remain consistent with the ledger. This approves the initial read-model strategy, not the final view definitions, query catalogue, indexes, refresh strategy for any future materialization, or implementation.

**D-14 decision record (2026-11-21):** The product owner approved **Option B** for account archiving: archived accounts are excluded from both Total Active Account Balance and Net Worth. To prevent an asset from disappearing from financial calculations without an economic event, an account may be archived only when its balance is zero in its own currency. A nonzero balance must first be settled through a valid transfer or other approved closing transaction; archiving must not itself write off, delete, or silently exclude a remaining balance. Posted journal history and historical transaction/report records remain preserved. Archived accounts reject new postings. Unarchive/reactivation permissions and flow remain a separate design detail; this decision does not authorize migrations or implementation.

**D-15 decision record (2026-11-21):** The product owner approved **Option A — Multiple Partial Refunds**. One eligible original expense may receive multiple linked partial refunds over time, or one full refund. The sum of successfully posted refunds must never exceed the original refundable amount; each new refund is validated against the remaining refundable balance. Refunds reduce/offset the original expense and are not income. Refund commands must lock the original transaction while calculating the remaining refundable amount, enforce idempotency and concurrency safety, and post atomically through the approved financial RPC boundary. Refunds remain separately posted business events and are not substitutes for reversing an erroneous transaction. Detailed multi-currency refund valuation, fees, return adjustments and refund-to-different-account cases remain G2 design deliverables. This decision does not authorize migrations or implementation.

**D-16 decision record (2026-11-21):** The product owner approved **Option A — Cash-like Positive Assets**, with the following explicit rules: eligible funds are based on active cash, bank and e-wallet accounts only; positive balances add to eligible funds and negative/overdraft balances reduce eligible funds. Credit-card available credit/limits, receivables and archived accounts are excluded. Pocket balances remain virtual allocations, not assets, and create no journal entries. `Unallocated = eligible funds − total allocated` is derived and may become negative after an expense or other balance change; display the shortfall clearly. New allocations that would increase an existing shortfall or cause total allocations to exceed eligible funds must be rejected; deallocation remains allowed. Foreign-currency account conversion into workspace base currency, valuation timestamp/rate and mixed-currency edge cases remain subject to D-18/D-19 and must not be guessed in implementation. **D-17.1 decision record (2026-11-21):** The product owner approved **Option A — Reject Overpayment**. A debt payment must be rejected when its total exceeds the remaining amount due under the debt obligation. For example, if the remaining obligation is Rp5,000,000, an attempted Rp6,000,000 payment is rejected and the user must enter a valid amount. The excess is not accepted as lender credit, a receivable/refundable balance, or an additional expense. This decision resolves overpayment handling only; early settlement, schedule changes and interest/fee treatment remain pending under D-17. It does not authorize migrations or implementation.


**D-17.2 decision record (2026-11-22):** The product owner approved **Option A — Early Settlement Using the Lender's Payoff Amount**. For early payoff, use the amount officially provided/quoted by the lender. The user records the payoff total and its components separately: remaining principal, interest, and any applicable fee or penalty. POCKA must not independently invent or calculate an interest discount, penalty, or payoff amount without an approved calculation rule and authoritative lender data. Once the settlement payment is successfully posted and the obligation is fully settled, mark the debt as settled and stop future installments. This decision does not resolve schedule edits or all detailed interest/fee rules, and it does not authorize migrations or implementation.


**D-17.3 decision record (2026-11-22):** The product owner approved **Option A — Schedule Changes Apply to Future Installments Only**. When a debt repayment schedule is edited, all already-paid installments, posted payments, journal entries and historical records remain unchanged. Only installments that have not yet been paid may be recalculated or rescheduled under the edited plan. A schedule edit must not rewrite, delete, reverse or silently reclassify posted financial history. This decision does not define every supported schedule-edit field or the detailed interest/fee recalculation rules; those remain design requirements under D-17. It does not authorize migrations or implementation.


**D-17.4 decision record (2026-11-22):** The product owner approved **Option A — One Total Payment Input with Automatic Allocation** for regular scheduled debt installments. During initial debt setup, the user records the debt terms and lender-provided repayment schedule/details needed to establish the principal, interest and applicable fee components. For each regular installment payment, the user enters only the total amount paid; POCKA automatically allocates that amount across principal, interest and fees using the previously configured, lender-backed schedule/terms. Principal reduces the outstanding debt liability; interest and fees are recorded as their respective expenses. The component amounts must be non-negative and reconcile exactly to the posted total, with money represented according to D-12 and posting performed through the D-11 RPC boundary. The user is not required to enter the components for each payment. POCKA must not invent interest, fees or an allocation when authoritative setup/schedule data is missing or insufficient; such cases require a defined exception flow rather than a guessed split. This decision concerns regular scheduled installments; D-17.2's lender-quoted payoff process for early settlement remains in force. Detailed amortization/calculation rules, irregular or partial payment allocation, rounding and lender schedule exceptions remain G2 design deliverables. This decision does not authorize migrations or implementation.


**D-17.5 decision record (2026-11-22):** The product owner approved **Option A — Allow Partial Installment Payments**. A user may pay less than the scheduled installment amount. POCKA records the actual amount received as a posted payment and keeps the unpaid remainder attached to that installment as still due; the installment status becomes **Partially Paid** until the remaining amount is paid or otherwise resolved under an approved lender-backed rule. The unpaid remainder must not be treated as paid, waived or deleted, and the system must continue to show it as outstanding. The payment's principal, interest and fee allocation must follow the configured lender-backed terms/schedule and the D-17.4 automatic-allocation rule; POCKA must not invent a proportional split or silently assume which component is paid first. Prevent cumulative payments from exceeding the amount due under the obligation, with concurrency-safe validation and the D-11 RPC boundary. The exact component-priority/partial-allocation method, due-date/late-fee treatment, carry-forward/rescheduling and lender-specific exceptions remain G2 design deliverables. This decision does not authorize migrations or implementation.


**D-17.6 decision record (2026-11-22):** The product owner approved **Option A — Lender-Provided Amortization Schedule as the Source of Truth**. During debt setup, the user provides or imports the lender's repayment/amortization schedule, including the scheduled principal, interest and applicable fee components where supplied. POCKA follows that schedule for installment amounts and component allocation; it does not independently calculate or replace lender-provided interest figures. This supports D-17.4's single total payment input and automatic allocation, and D-17.5's partial-payment handling only to the extent the lender schedule provides a valid basis for allocation. If the schedule is missing, incomplete or insufficient to determine a payment's component split, POCKA must not invent the interest calculation or guessed allocation; the payment requires a defined exception/manual reconciliation flow. Early settlement continues to use the lender-quoted payoff under D-17.2. Schedule import format, validation, updates/revisions, partial-payment allocation where lender data does not specify it, and exception handling remain G2 design deliverables. This decision does not authorize migrations or implementation.


**D-17.7 decision record (2026-11-22):** The product owner approved **Option A — Import a Revised Schedule from the Lender**. When the lender formally changes a repayment schedule (for example, following restructuring or a lender-approved term change), the user provides or imports the revised lender schedule. The revised schedule applies only to unpaid/future installments from its documented effective point; paid installments, posted payment transactions, journal entries and other historical records remain unchanged. POCKA preserves the prior and revised schedule versions, their source/provenance and effective date so the change is auditable. The system must not silently rewrite paid history or independently invent revised lender terms. Import format, validation, mapping of revised installments to outstanding obligations, effective-date rules and handling of already partially paid installments remain G2 design deliverables. This decision does not authorize migrations or implementation.


**D-17.8 decision record (2026-11-22):** The product owner approved **Option A — Preserve the Existing Partial Payment and Outstanding Remainder**. When a revised lender schedule is received for an installment that has already been partially paid, keep every posted payment and its historical principal/interest/fee allocation unchanged. Preserve the unpaid remainder as an outstanding obligation; do not reset the installment to unpaid, duplicate the original amount, erase the payment or treat the remainder as paid. The revised lender schedule must explicitly account for or reconcile that remaining obligation before POCKA applies revised future installment terms. POCKA must not invent how the remainder is redistributed or allocated if the lender's revised schedule does not specify it; use a defined exception/manual reconciliation flow. Retain links between the prior installment, posted partial payment, outstanding remainder and revised schedule version for auditability. Exact lender reconciliation rules, installment mapping and any revised component allocation remain G2 design deliverables. This decision does not authorize migrations or implementation.


**D-17.9 decision record (2026-11-22):** The product owner approved **Option B — POCKA Calculates an Estimated Component Allocation** when a lender's revised schedule provides the installment total but omits the principal/interest/fee breakdown. In this specific case, POCKA may calculate an estimated allocation using the interest rate and remaining tenor supplied/confirmed by the user, together with the outstanding debt information already recorded. The result must be clearly identified as a **POCKA estimate**, not a lender-provided or lender-confirmed breakdown; it must not overwrite the lender's total installment amount or any component figures the lender did provide. The estimate must not be presented as an official lender allocation. If required inputs or a reliable calculation basis are missing, the system must not fabricate values and must use an exception/manual reconciliation flow. The exact interest method, rate convention, payment timing, fee handling, rounding, schedule validation, estimate disclosure/confirmation and accounting-posting treatment remain G2 design deliverables. This decision is a narrow exception to D-17.6 only for lender schedules that omit component details; it does not authorize POCKA to override lender-provided component figures. This decision does not authorize migrations or implementation.


**D-17.10 decision record (2026-11-22):** The product owner approved **Option A — User Confirmation Required Before Posting an Estimated Allocation**. When POCKA calculates an estimated principal/interest/fee split under D-17.9 because the lender omitted component details, it must present the estimated breakdown and clearly identify it as a POCKA estimate. The user must review and explicitly confirm the proposed allocation before the related payment can be posted. The estimate must not be posted automatically or silently. Confirmation does not turn the estimate into a lender-confirmed figure; retain its estimated status and provenance in the resulting financial/audit records. If the user does not confirm, or required calculation inputs are insufficient, do not post using that estimate; use the defined exception/manual reconciliation flow. The exact review UI, confirmation capture, estimate metadata, calculation method and RPC contract remain G2 design deliverables. This decision does not authorize migrations or implementation.


**D-17.11 decision record (2026-11-22):** The product owner approved **Option B — Users May Manually Edit POCKA-Estimated Components Before Confirmation**. When POCKA generates an estimated principal/interest/fee breakdown under D-17.9, the user may edit the estimated component amounts before confirming the payment. The edited split must reconcile exactly to the lender's scheduled total (or actual payment amount where the applicable approved partial-payment rules govern); monetary values continue to follow D-12. Any user-edited breakdown must be identified and retained as a **user-adjusted POCKA estimate**, with the original POCKA estimate, edited values, editor and confirmation/audit provenance preserved. It must never be represented as lender-provided or lender-confirmed. D-17.10 remains in force: explicit user review and confirmation are required before posting. This edit permission applies only to components POCKA estimated because lender details were absent; it must not modify or override component figures actually provided by the lender. Calculation basis, validation, exact audit metadata and RPC contract remain G2 design deliverables. This decision does not authorize migrations or implementation.


## 7. Approved UX principles

**D-37 decision record (2026-11-22):** The product owner approved **Simple by default, advanced when needed** as the governing UX principle for POCKA Debt. The ordinary debt setup and repayment journeys must ask for only the information necessary for the current step and present a clear, guided default path. Advanced lender/schedule details, estimate review and editing, schedule revisions, partial-payment reconciliation and exception handling should appear progressively when the user's situation requires them, rather than being exposed as one dense form or accounting-heavy workflow. The interface must not hide or bypass information required for correct posting: lender-backed schedule data remains the source of truth under D-17.6; POCKA estimates remain clearly labelled and require explicit review/confirmation under D-17.9–D-17.11; insufficient data must lead to a clear exception/manual-reconciliation path, never a guessed posting. Keep principal/interest/fee accounting invisible in the normal path wherever possible while showing a clear payment total, status and remaining amount due. This is a UX presentation principle only; it does not relax financial invariants, authorize fabricated data, change approved debt semantics, or authorize implementation.


**D-17.12 decision record (2026-11-22):** The product owner approved **Option A — Manual Reconciliation** for partial-installment payments when the lender's authoritative schedule/terms do not specify how a partial payment is allocated across principal, interest and fees. POCKA must not invent a proportional split, assume a component-priority order, or automatically derive an allocation without an approved lender-backed basis. The user must provide/reconcile the component allocation using authoritative lender information before the payment can be posted. The component amounts must be non-negative and sum exactly to the actual payment amount under D-12; posting remains subject to D-11 RPC-only mutation, remaining-obligation validation and concurrency protection. If the user cannot obtain or substantiate the allocation, keep the payment unposted and direct the user to a clear exception/manual-reconciliation state; do not silently post an incomplete or guessed split. This decision resolves only the missing allocation rule for partial payments; lender-specific evidence requirements, UX details, audit provenance and RPC contract remain G2 design deliverables. It does not authorize migrations or implementation.


**D-17.13 decision record (2026-11-22):** The product owner approved **Option A — Overdue Status and Late Fees Follow Lender-Confirmed Information**. An installment becomes **Overdue** when its due date has passed and it remains unpaid; if it has received a partial payment but still has an unpaid remainder after the due date, show **Partially Paid — Overdue**. POCKA must not invent or independently calculate a late fee without an approved rule and authoritative lender information. When the lender has not yet confirmed the late-fee amount, show it as **Not Confirmed** rather than treating it as zero. Record a lender-confirmed late fee as a separate fee/expense component, distinct from principal and scheduled interest, with its source/provenance and confirmation details retained. If the lender later revises the fee, preserve the change history and do not rewrite posted payments or historical journal entries. Exact lender evidence, fee posting/account mapping, due-date/timezone boundary, grace-period treatment, notification behaviour and RPC/audit contracts remain G2 design deliverables. This decision does not authorize migrations or implementation.


**D-17.14 decision record (2026-11-22):** The product owner approved **Option A — Smart Reminder with Default Schedule** for debt notifications. In-app reminders are scheduled for three days before an installment due date (H-3), one day before (H-1), and on the due date (H). If an installment remains unpaid after its due date, send an overdue reminder when it becomes overdue and repeat every three days while an amount remains outstanding; stop overdue reminders when the installment is fully paid or otherwise no longer due under an approved resolution. Notify the user when a lender confirms a new late fee, without implying a fee exists while it is still **Not Confirmed**. After a payment posts successfully, provide an in-app confirmation and show any remaining unpaid amount/status. Users may adjust or disable debt reminders. V1 uses in-app notifications only; email, push and other channels are outside this decision and require separate approval. Notifications must never create or initiate a payment or financial posting automatically. Exact timezone/due-date scheduling, deduplication, delivery/retry, preference defaults and event/RPC integration remain implementation design deliverables. This decision does not authorize migrations or implementation.


**D-17.15 decision record (2026-11-22):** The product owner confirmed **Option A — Aging Status Only**. POCKA groups each unpaid installment by elapsed overdue duration: **1–30 days**, **31–60 days**, **61–90 days**, and **more than 90 days** (Long-term Overdue). This is an informational aging classification only. It does not create a separate escalation workflow, assert a legal/default/credit-status classification, change lender terms, alter the amount owed, waive or write off debt, or change the repayment schedule. A partially paid installment remains in the applicable aging band while an unpaid remainder remains outstanding; a fully paid or otherwise resolved installment is no longer aged as outstanding. Aging is calculated from the installment's due date using elapsed days overdue, not calendar-month labels. Existing D-17.14 reminders continue to apply; D-17.15 adds no extra alerts or escalation notifications. **D-17.16 decision record (2026-11-22):** The product owner approved **Option B — Automatic Principal Reduction for Extra Repayments**. Because POCKA V1 is not integrated with lenders, a user-recorded extra repayment outside the scheduled installment is treated by POCKA as a principal-reducing payment by default. Once the payment is successfully posted, reduce the outstanding principal by the extra amount and recalculate the remaining repayment schedule within POCKA. Record the extra repayment as a separate financial event linked to the debt; do not misclassify it as an ordinary scheduled installment. This is POCKA's internal debt model and must not be represented as lender-confirmed treatment or a guarantee that the lender's own balance/schedule has changed. Preserve the original schedule and posted history; retain an auditable schedule version/change record for the recalculated future installments. The exact recalculation method, treatment of accrued interest/fees, effective date, rounding, partial extra repayments and lender-specific differences remain G2 design deliverables. This decision does not authorize migrations or implementation.
